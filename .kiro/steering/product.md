# Rewind 1970: Product and Tone

## One-liner
Rewind 1970 turns journaling into a haunted exploration. Each memory the user writes becomes a VHS tape hidden somewhere in a dark house. Tapes that are neglected decay, and the user has to find them and rewind them before they fade.

## Hackathon context
- Event: Kiroween. Category: Costume Contest. The haunted interface is how the app works, not decoration on top of it.
- Deadline: December 4. Target: December 3.
- The Kiro specs, steering files and hooks are part of what judges review. Keep them accurate and up to date as the code changes.

## Tone rules
- Spooky, never gore. Suggestion over shock.
- No blood, wounds, body horror or jump scares with loud audio stings.
- Fear comes from things that are slightly wrong: an impossible date, an object that moved, a shape seen only for a moment under lightning.
- The heart of the app is soft. The horror exists to bring forgotten memories back, not to punish the user.
- Never mock or judge what the user writes. Tape decay is a visual effect only. It must never delete or alter the stored memory text.

## Core experience
1. Cold open: a found-tape boot screen dated 1970-10-31 00:00, a date before VHS existed.
2. Outside: the house in a storm. Lightning briefly reveals hidden things.
3. Inside: the house is the interface. There are no menus; exploring is navigating the journal.
4. Tapes: memories hidden around the house. They decay with neglect and are restored on the living-room TV.
5. The pond: still water under a blood moon, with a skeleton glimpsed beneath it only during lightning or after ripples.
6. Endings: decided by which tapes were found and how healthy they are. The last tape is one the user never recorded.

## Copy voice
- Short, plain and quiet. Use lowercase for diegetic text such as tape labels and on-screen prompts.
- Use the language of the era of tape technology: PLAY, REW, TRACKING, EJECT.
- Never explain the mystery of the 1970 date in the UI.

## Privacy
- Memories never leave the device. No accounts, analytics or network requests that contain user content.
